Privacy

This page is in plain words. It is a description of what the code does, not legal advice.

What happens to your camera

When you press Start camera, the browser gives this page a live video stream from your webcam. Each frame is measured inside the tab, in your browser's memory, to work out where your face is and how it is moving. The results are a set of numbers, not a picture.

When you close or reload the page, the video stream and those numbers are gone. The neutral face you set with Set neutral face is only held in memory too, so it is forgotten on reload. There is no save button, no record button and no screenshot feature.

What never happens

  • Your camera frames are never uploaded anywhere.
  • They are never stored, logged, or written to disk.
  • They are never recorded, and there is no code to record them.
  • You are never identified, and no attempt is made to recognise you.
  • No profile, score or history is built about you.

What leaves your device

Nothing, by default. The face model and its WebAssembly files are served from this site's own address, so loading the model sends no information about you anywhere else.

The one exception is the photo search, and it is off until you tick its box. If you tick Show photos of this expression, this page sends a short text search phrase such as surprised face to the Wikimedia Commons application programming interface. Wikimedia receives that phrase and your IP address, as any web server does, and its own privacy policy applies from that point on. Nothing about your face, camera or camera settings is part of that request. Ticking the box off again stops all of it immediately.

Cookies and similar storage

None are used. There is no cookie, no localStorage, no sessionStorage, no IndexedDB, no analytics, no tracking pixel and no third-party font or script. Nothing about your visit is recorded by this site.

Because no cookie or similar storage is used, there is no consent banner here and none is needed. If that ever changes, a consent step will be added before anything is stored or sent.

Facial data and the law

Facial information is treated as sensitive personal data. Indonesia's Personal Data Protection Law (Law No. 27 of 2022) treats biometric data as a specific category of personal data, and the EU's General Data Protection Regulation treats biometric data used to identify a person the same way.

This app does not identify anyone. It measures the movement of a face and compares it with a list of fixed expressions, and it throws the numbers away. It is built to keep nothing precisely so that there is no facial data to lose, leak or hand over. That is a design choice, not a legal claim.

Your camera frames stay in this tab. Nothing is uploaded, stored or recorded, and there are no cookies. If you turn photo lookup on, a short search phrase and your IP address go to Wikimedia Commons.
