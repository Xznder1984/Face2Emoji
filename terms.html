Face to Emoji

Terms

This page is provided as it is. There is no account, no charge and no contract to agree to. Using the page means you accept the points below.

The match is approximate

This app measures how your face is moving and picks the emoji whose rule matches those measurements. It does not read feelings, and it is not always right. Lighting, your position, your camera and a weak connection all affect what it can see. A matched emoji is a guess about facial movement, nothing more.

What it must not be used for

Do not use this app for:

  • medical, diagnostic or health decisions of any kind;
  • psychological assessment, screening or diagnosis;
  • security, access control, surveillance or monitoring;
  • identifying, confirming or denying who a person is.

Photos belong to their creators

If you turn photo lookup on, the photos shown come from Wikimedia Commons and belong to the people who made them, under the licences shown next to each photo. Those licences are the only permission you have. Following the link to the original page, reading the licence and complying with it is your responsibility, including attribution and any share-alike terms. This app does not endorse those photos and cannot guarantee what a licence actually allows.

Other people

Do not point the camera at someone without their consent, and do not publish anything that shows another person's face using this app.

Availability

The page is offered as it is, with no promise that it will always be available or error free. Nothing is sold, so there is nothing to refund.

What it does with your camera

Camera frames are processed in your browser tab and are never uploaded, stored or recorded. The privacy page says exactly what happens.

Your camera frames stay in this tab. Nothing is uploaded, stored or recorded, and there are no cookies. If you turn photo lookup on, a short search phrase and your IP address go to Wikimedia Commons.
